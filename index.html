The 5-Phase Social Friction Matrix

The algorithmic sequence through which high-friction micro-communities neutralize non-conformist actors.

FAZ 1

Radar: Deviation Detection

"Why are you pushing so hard? The pass grade is fixed anyway."

  • Target: Detect deviation and signal the minimum comfort boundary.
  • Mechanism: Friendly inquiry masking subtle norm-enforcement.
  • Counter-Stance: Calm persistence; avoid entering philosophical debates.
FAZ 2

Ridicule: Banter & Caricature

"Look at our genius saving the department again!"

  • Target: Erode the outlier's self-esteem and drag standards to the mean.
  • Mechanism: Sarcastic jokes framed as playful camaraderie.
  • Counter-Stance: Complete non-defensiveness; refuse to explain or justify.
FAZ 3

Cabal: Alliance & Gossip

"There's something off about them lately... they think they're better."

  • Target: Unify the ingroup against the outlier through mutual validation.
  • Mechanism: Backchannel whisper networks and character devaluation.
  • Counter-Stance: Cut emotional dependencies; secure confidential data channels.
FAZ 4

Isolation: Silent Ostracism

*(Conversations abruptly stop upon entry; dropped eye contact)*

  • Target: Erase the outlier ontologically; trigger dACC neurological pain.
  • Mechanism: Total information blackout and collective communicative silence.
  • Counter-Stance: Zero emotional reaction; invest 100% of bandwidth into external ecosystems.
FAZ 5

Liquidation: Exit or Assimilation

"They were never a fit for our culture anyway."

  • Target: Permanently eliminate the psychological threat of the mirror.
  • Mechanism: Outlier departs or undergoes complete cognitive capitulation.
  • Counter-Stance: Hirschman Strategic Exit: Leap to a high-mobility macro-market.
